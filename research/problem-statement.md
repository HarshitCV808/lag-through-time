Round 1 Problem Statement - Intel 1 Million Minds
Gen Z comprises roughly around 30% of India’s population and they already use AI
every day, but they don’t believe they need an AI PC. Can your game turn “Why an AI
PC?” into “I want this Intel AI PC”? Vibe-code an irresistible in-store experience that
lets Gen-Z shoppers play, discover and choose Intel over the competition.
Problem: Intel has introduced AI PCs that can enable more responsive, personalized,
and efficient AI experiences for everyday work and creativity. Yet there is an adoption
and experience problem. People who walk into retails stores get carried away by PCs
with less price and fancy colors and other non-functional aesthetic aspects, while
choosing a PC which is not meant for long term performance.
Your Task: Vibe-code an exciting, interactive game that helps shoppers discover Intel-
powered AI PCs and makes a compelling case to buy one over competitors. Design this
for walk-in customers at Large Format Retail (LFR) stores such as Reliance Digital,
Croma and Vijay Sales - assume zero to less prior technical knowledge. Create a short,
intuitive experience that grabs attention within seconds and delivers its core message in
2–3 minutes. You may want to focus on one or all of AI PC features into playable
challenges spanning distinctive use cases in learning, work, creativity, gaming and
everyday life. Depending on the use case, you may showcase AI on-device application
support, privacy, battery life and performance.
Make Intel’s advantages tangible compared to key competitors. End with a personalised
AI PC recommendation and a clear next step.
Evaluation Criteria (10 points)
1. 2. 3. 4. Creativity of the game (3 points)
Persuasive Impact towards buying (3 points)
Technical accuracy of AI PC features (3 points)
Showcase of competitive superiority (1 point)
